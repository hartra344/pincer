Open **Settings → Appearance** to choose how Pincer looks.

## Pincer Settings

Pincer's own Settings hold preferences for this device. They're separate from [Gateway Settings](../gateway-settings/), which change the gateway's config.

- **macOS:** choose **Pincer → Settings…** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>). Settings is a window with tabs.
- **iPhone and iPad:** tap the gear at the top of the chat list, or open the [command palette](../command-palette-and-navigation/) (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) and choose **Open Settings…**. Settings opens a list of pages; tap one to open it.

Both have the same pages, in this order:

| Page | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Your name; **Launch**, **Quick Capture** and **Menu Bar** (macOS only); **Storage** (cached chats and the outbox); **Search** (Spotlight); **Tips**; **About** (help and the privacy policy). |
| **Gateways** | The gateways saved on this device, their connection settings, the demo and **This device**. See [Manage gateways](../manage-gateways/). |
| **Appearance** | Theme, avatars, colors and **Sidebar**. |
| **Chats** | Thinking steps and reactions; **Read Aloud**; **Dictation**. |
| **Notifications** | Notifications on this device and, on iOS, [while Pincer is closed](../push-notifications/). |
| **Privacy** | **Location**: **Share location context with the agent** (see [Composer](../composer/)). **Web Content**: **Load images the agent links from the web**. |
| **Keyboard Shortcuts** | iPad and Mac only (the Mac tab is labeled **Shortcuts**). See [Change a shortcut](../../reference/keyboard-shortcuts/#change-a-shortcut). |

On macOS, the Settings window fits the available height of its display. Moving it to a shorter display, or changing the space available around the Dock, updates the height limit; long tabs scroll and shorter tabs stay compact.

## Light, Dark or System

Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device.

## Themes

Themes color the accent, links, both avatars, and the chat, sidebar and code backgrounds, with separate shades for light and dark mode. Code-block Copy and Preview buttons update their accent immediately when the theme changes.

**Default** keeps Pincer's original look: your system accent color and the standard backgrounds.

## Custom colors

Any of a theme's colors can be overridden with your own pick, and reset back to the theme's.

## Sidebar

The **Sidebar** section sets what the chat list shows:

- **Show last message under each chat**: a one-line preview of the latest message. On by default.
- **List subagent runs under their chat**: off by default, so each chat is one thread and you open a run from its tool call. See [Organizing chats](../organizing-chats/).

## Chat and group icons and colors

You can also style individual chats and groups from the sidebar:

- **Color → Custom…** on a chat picks any color.
- **Change Icon…** on a chat or group header picks an SF Symbol.

These sync to your other devices through the gateway. See [Organizing chats](../organizing-chats/).
