Open **Settings → Appearance** to choose how Pincer looks.

## Light, Dark or System

Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device.

## Themes

Themes color the accent, links, both avatars, and the chat, sidebar and code backgrounds, with separate shades for light and dark mode.

**Default** keeps Pincer's original look: your system accent color and the standard backgrounds.

## Custom colors

Any of a theme's colors can be overridden with your own pick, and reset back to the theme's.

## Chat and group icons and colors

You can also style individual chats and groups from the sidebar:

- **Color → Custom…** on a chat picks any color.
- **Change Icon…** on a chat or group header picks an SF Symbol.

These sync to your other devices through the gateway. See [Organizing chats](../organizing-chats/).
