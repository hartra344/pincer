## Sending messages

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Send | <kbd>Return</kbd> |
| New line | <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> or <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> |
| Stop the current run | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> |

<kbd>Return</kbd> sends when you type on a hardware keyboard: on a Mac, and on iPad or iPhone with a keyboard attached. On the on-screen keyboard, <kbd>Return</kbd> starts a new line, and you send with the **Send** button.

<kbd>Return</kbd> does nothing when the message can't be sent, such as when it's empty, or it's a slash command while you're offline (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> still starts a new line). While you're typing with an input method that composes text, such as Japanese or Chinese, <kbd>Return</kbd> confirms the candidate first; press it again to send.

If you're offline, or a message can't be sent, it stays in the chat marked **Queued** or **Failed — Retry**. See [Offline sending & retry](../offline-outbox/).

## Replying to a message

After you choose **Reply** on a message (see [Replies](../transcript/#replies)), a **Replying to** chip sits above the composer with who wrote the message and how it starts. The composer takes focus, so you can start typing. Send as usual and your message is sent as a reply to that one, and the chip goes away.

- To cancel, click the ✕ in the chip, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>. When slash command suggestions are showing, <kbd>Esc</kbd> hides them first.
- Choosing **Reply** on another message replaces the one you're replying to.
- Each chat keeps its own reply while you switch between chats. Replies aren't saved in drafts, so they're gone after you quit Pincer.
- Slash commands aren't sent as replies. The chip stays for your next message.
- Replies you send while offline are queued with the rest of your messages and still go out as replies. See [Offline sending & retry](../offline-outbox/).

The gateway gives the agent the message you replied to, so your text is sent as you wrote it. Older gateways don't accept replies. With those, Pincer quotes the original at the start of your message instead, as `> **Claw:** …`, and keeps doing so on that connection.

## Attachments

Paste, drag in, or pick images and files with the **+** button. Images are downscaled to fit the gateway's limits before they're sent.

While you're offline, attachments queue with their message and go out when you reconnect, even after quitting Pincer, up to the outbox's size limit. Over the limit, the composer says **Too large to queue offline — sends when you’re connected** and keeps the message in your draft. See [Offline sending & retry](../offline-outbox/#attachments-in-the-outbox).

To send something from another app, use the share sheet. See [Share to Pincer](../sharing-to-pincer/).

## Dictation

Click or tap the **microphone** button next to the text box and start talking. Your words appear in the text box as you speak. They go where your cursor is. If you've selected text, your words replace it once Pincer hears them; if you say nothing, the selection stays. If the text box isn't focused when you start, they go at the end. Pincer never sends a dictated message on its own: read it over, fix anything, then send it as usual.

You can also press <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> (**Dictate Message**), or choose **Dictate Message** (**Stop Dictation** while listening) in the [command palette](../command-palette-and-navigation/). On an iPad, the shortcut works with a hardware keyboard. In split view, it toggles the pane you're using. To change it, see [Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/keyboard-shortcuts/#change-a-shortcut).

Dictation stops when you:

- click or tap the microphone again,
- press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, which keeps the text,
- start typing, or
- send the message or switch to another chat. The text is kept as it is.

When you send while still listening, Pincer waits a moment for the last words to be recognized, so they're included in the message.

Starting dictation stops [Read Aloud](../read-aloud/) if a reply is being read.

The first time, Pincer asks for permission to use the **microphone** and **speech recognition**. If you said no, the microphone button shows why and offers **Open Settings**, where you can allow it. On a Mac, that's **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

:::note
Unless you turn on **On-device only** (below), your device may send the audio to Apple to turn it into text. It isn't sent to your gateway. Only the text you send reaches the gateway.
:::

To keep your voice on the device, turn on **Settings → Conversation → Dictation → On-device only**. When on, your speech is recognized on this device and isn't sent to Apple. When off, Apple's servers may be used for languages that don't have an on-device model. If your language has no on-device model while it's on, Settings says so ("On-device dictation isn't available for *language*, so dictation won't work while this is on."), and the microphone button explains it and doesn't dictate. Turn the setting off to use Apple's servers.

The microphone button doesn't appear if speech recognition isn't available for your language.

## Drafts

Each chat keeps what you haven't sent yet, both the text and any attachments, when you switch to another chat or quit Pincer. Come back and it's still there.

- Drafts are saved a moment after you stop typing.
- A draft is deleted when you send it, when its chat is deleted, or when you remove its gateway.
- Drafts stay on this device. They don't sync.

Where drafts are stored, and how to turn them off, is in [Security & privacy](../../reference/security/#drafts).

## Context meter

A small ring next to **Send** shows how full the chat's context window is. It appears once the gateway has reported how many tokens the chat uses, and a limit is known.

- The ring turns **orange** at 85% and **red** at 95%, and shows the percentage inside from 85%.
- Hover over it for the numbers, such as "Context: 172k / 200k tokens (86%)".
- A "~" means the number is an estimate from before the last run. It doesn't change color.

Click the ring for details:

- **Context Window** (or **Prompt Budget**, when the session has one): tokens used, the limit and the percentage;
- a hint when it's filling up. At 95% the agent will compact on its own soon;
- **Last run:** tokens in and out;
- **Instructions (optional)** and **Compact Now**.

### Compact Now

**Compact Now** summarizes older messages to free up room, like OpenClaw does on its own when the context is full. Add instructions to tell it what to keep. When it's done, Pincer shows the result, such as "Compacted 172k → 31k tokens."

**Compact Now** is greyed out while the agent is working. Wait for the current run to finish.

With **Full Management** and no instructions, Pincer asks the gateway to compact the session directly. Otherwise it sends `/compact`, or `/compact` followed by your instructions, as a message. Either way it stays the same chat. See [Compaction](../organizing-chats/#compaction).

## Slash commands

Type `/` to see the commands the gateway offers for this chat: built-ins, skills and plugins. After you pick one, Pincer suggests its arguments too:

- listed choices, such as `/verbose on`;
- the agent's models, for `/model`;
- the session's thinking levels, for `/think`.

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move through suggestions | <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> |
| Complete | <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Return</kbd> |
| Hide suggestions | <kbd>Esc</kbd> |
| Send a complete command | <kbd>Return</kbd> |

A few notes:

- `/clear` is sent as `/reset`, like the Control UI.
- Gateways that don't support `commands.list` get a built-in list of common commands.

On Mac, when suggestions are closed, <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> move to the next or previous keyboard control. Use <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> to insert a tab in the draft. While suggestions are open, plain <kbd>Tab</kbd> completes the selected command.

### Handy commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/reasoning on` | Save and stream the agent's reasoning for this session. |
| `/model` | Switch the session's model. |
| `/think` | Set the session's thinking level. |
| `/compact` | Summarize older messages to free up context. Add instructions after it to say what to keep. |
| `/reset` or `/clear` | Start the session fresh. |

The exact list depends on your gateway, its skills and its plugins.
