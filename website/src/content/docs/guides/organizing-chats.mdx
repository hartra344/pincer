The sidebar keeps your chats organized by server, channel and agent. This page covers how it's laid out and how to arrange it.

## Layouts

Pick a layout from the menu at the top of the sidebar:

| Layout | How chats are grouped |
| --- | --- |
| **By server** (default) | Each Discord server gets a section with its `#channels`. Automations get their own section when shown, and everything else is grouped under its agent. |
| **By agent** | One section per agent, with its groups nested inside (see [Groups in By agent](#groups-in-by-agent)). Every agent's home chat is listed first, even before its first message. |
| **By group** | One section per group you've made. |
| **Recent** | One list, most recently active first. |

### The Organize menu

This menu, called **Organize**, also has:

- **Show Archived**, to list archived chats;
- **Show Automations** and **Show Slash Commands**, to list automation (cron) chats and slash-command chats (see [Automation and slash-command chats](#automation-and-slash-command-chats));
- **New Group…**, in *By group* and *By server*;
- **Automations…**;
- **Approval History…**, to see [past approval decisions](../gateway-settings/#approval-history);
- **Gateway Settings…** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>);
- **Edit Connection…**, which opens the **Connection** page of Gateway Settings;
- **Reconnect**.

### Discord server names

Server names come from your gateway's Discord config (`channels.discord.guilds.<id>.slug`) when it's set. Otherwise the section is just called **Discord**.

To name a server yourself, right-click its header and choose **Rename Server…**. The name syncs to your other devices through the gateway.

:::note
Discord doesn't send its categories to the gateway. To recreate them, use [groups](#groups).
:::

## Groups

Groups are your own folders for chats. They sync through the gateway, so they're the same on every device.

- **Create a group:** choose **New Group…** from the Organize menu or a group header's menu, or right-click a chat and choose **Move to Group → New Group…**.
- **Move chats:** drag a chat onto a group (or onto **Ungrouped**). Drop it between two chats to put it at that spot. In *By server*, dropping a grouped chat on its own server or agent takes it back out of the group.
- **Reorder groups:** drag a group header, or use **Move Up** and **Move Down** in its menu.
- **Icon:** **Change Icon…** on a group's header picks an SF Symbol.
- **Delete:** **Delete Group…** removes the group and leaves its chats ungrouped.

Empty groups stay until you delete them, so you can set them up ahead of time.

VoiceOver names a plain group header’s **+** button with its group, such as “New chat in Home” or “New chat in Personal”, so the action has context when navigating the sidebar.

### Groups in By agent

In *By agent*, each agent's section keeps your groups as a second level:

- the agent's **home chat** comes first, followed by its pinned chats that aren't in a group;
- then each group that has at least one of that agent's chats, in the same order as in *By group*;
- then the agent's chats that aren't in a group.

A group with chats from several agents shows up under each of them, with only that agent's chats. Each group under each agent collapses on its own, and the sidebar remembers it.

When you open a chat from the command palette (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), with **Next Unread Chat**, from a notification or from a `pincer://` link, and it's inside a collapsed agent or group, the sidebar expands them so you can see where it lives. Renaming a group keeps it collapsed or expanded, in every view.

On iPhone and iPad the sidebar shows the same tree as on the Mac:

- **Agents** are bold section headers with the agent's avatar. Tap the chevron to collapse an agent; a collapsed agent shows a red badge with its unread count.
- **Groups** are rows with a folder (or the group's own icon) and the number of chats in them. Tap the chevron to collapse a group; a collapsed group with unread chats shows the unread count instead.
- **Chats in a group** are indented under it, so you can tell at a glance which chats belong to which group.

VoiceOver reads each agent and group with its level and whether it's expanded or collapsed, for example "Preparations, group in Mochi, 2 chats, expanded".

- **New chat in a group:** click **+** on a group under an agent, or right-click the agent and choose **New Chat in Group**. The chat goes to that agent and that group.
- **Move chats:** drag a chat onto another of the agent's groups, or between two of its chats to put it at that spot. Drop it on the agent's header to take it out of its group. Moving a chat between groups never changes its agent, so you can't drop it into another agent's group.
- A group's header menu has the same actions as in *By group*: **Change Icon…**, **Rename…** and **Delete Group…** apply to the group everywhere. To reorder groups, switch to *By group*.

In the built-in demo, **Claw** has **Home** and **Personal** groups, **Scout** has **Reading** and **Work**, and **Mochi**, a New York moving planner, has **Preparations** and **Day of move** groups plus a chat outside them.

## Per-chat actions

Right-click a chat (or long-press on iOS) for:

- **Pin**, to keep it at the top;
- **Rename**;
- **Move to Group**;
- **Color**, from OpenClaw's named colors, or **Custom…** for any color;
- **Change Icon…**, to pick an SF Symbol tinted with the chat's color, or **Reset Icon**;
- **Reasoning level**;
- **Archive**.

Custom colors and icons sync to your other devices. See [Synced preferences](../../reference/synced-preferences/).

## New chats, forks and branches

Chats you start with **New Chat** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>) are listed on their own, like in the Control UI. Only forks and branches nest under their parent.

## Working chats

While a chat has a run going, its agent's avatar dances at the right of the row, in place of the date: a small hop and wiggle that loops about once a second. It stops and disappears when the run ends.

The avatar follows **Settings → Appearance → Animated avatars**: with it on you see the agent's pet, and with it off you see the agent's emoji or initial on a colored disc. With **Reduce Motion** on, the avatar stays still and a small accent-colored dot at its corner shows the chat is working.

Hover the avatar (macOS) for who's working, for example "Moki is working". VoiceOver reads the same after the chat's name.

When a chat isn't running, its row shows how recently it was active. A timestamp slightly ahead of your device's clock appears as **now**.

## Unread chats

A chat with new messages shows its agent's pet at the right of the row with a small accent-colored badge in the corner. While the agent is idle the pet does a gentle hop every couple of seconds, then rests; while a run is going it dances nonstop, so you can still tell working chats from ones that are just waiting to be read. Pets only move while the Pincer window is active, and with **Reduce Motion** on they stay still. If helpers are working, the badge shows their count instead.

With **Animated avatars** off, idle unread chats show a plain dot. VoiceOver reads "Unread" after the chat's name either way. Helper runs never show as unread.

A reply that arrives in the chat you're looking at is marked read straight away. If Pincer is in the background or its window isn't focused, the chat stays unread (and notifies you) until you come back to it. On iPhone, the chat counts as open only while it's actually on screen, not just selected in the list.

## Helper (subagent) runs

Subagent runs stay out of the sidebar by default. When helpers are working, the parent chat's agent avatar dances with a small count badge (for example "Moki: 2 helper runs working"), and you open one from the **Open run** button on the tool call that started it.

If you'd rather see them, **Settings → Sidebar** can list them under their parent, behind a ✨ count chip. Helper runs never add to unread counts or send notifications.

To see every helper a chat started, with its status and a timeline of what it did, open the chat's [Runs panel](../subagents-and-runs/).

## Automation and slash-command chats

Chats created by automations (cron jobs) and by slash commands are hidden from the sidebar by default. Turn on **Show Automations** or **Show Slash Commands** in the Organize menu to list them in every layout. Each gateway remembers its own choice.

While hidden, they don't add to unread counts or section badges. They're still easy to reach:

- the chat you have open stays in the sidebar until you leave it;
- typing in the sidebar's **Search** field lists matching hidden chats too;
- [Search messages](../search/), the command palette (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), links and **Organize → Automations…** open them as usual.

## Compaction

When a chat's context is compacted, it stays the same chat. You'll see a "Compacting context…" line while it runs, then a divider in the same thread.

To see how full the context is, or to compact it yourself, use the [context meter](../composer/#context-meter) next to **Send**.

## Finding your way

- **Search** at the top of the sidebar filters chats by name. While you type, a **Search messages for “…”** row above the chat list searches the text of every cached chat instead. See [Search messages](../search/).
- **Unread badges** (the agent's pet with a badge, or a dot) and the **Dock badge** show what's new. See [Unread chats](#unread-chats).
- **Next Unread Chat** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>) jumps to the next chat with unread messages.
- The **command palette** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) finds any chat on any gateway by typing a few letters.
- **Back** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>[</kbd>) and **Forward** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>]</kbd>) retrace the chats you've visited.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd> open your first nine pinned chats.

See [Command palette & navigation](../command-palette-and-navigation/).

### Quick switching

Press **⌘K** and type part of a chat name. Matching is fuzzy: in the demo, **jptr** finds **Japan trip**. Press Return to open the selected result, or Escape to close the palette.

## Automations

Choose **Organize → Automations…** (or **Manage Automations…** on the Automations section's header) to open the gateway's cron jobs: a window on macOS and a sheet on iOS. Each job shows its schedule, last and next run, and status; a failing job shows its error. Select one to see its task, delivery destination, and run history (`cron.runs`), where every run links to its chat.

**Run Now**, **Pause**/**Resume**, **Edit**, **New** and **Delete** (`cron.run`, `cron.update`, `cron.add`, `cron.remove`) require **Access → Full Management**. If a job was edited elsewhere in the meantime, Pincer refuses your change instead of overwriting it. The list updates live from the gateway's `cron` events. On gateways without `cron.*`, Pincer says automations aren't available.
