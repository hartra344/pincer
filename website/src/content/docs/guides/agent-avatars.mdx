Each agent can have a small companion pet as its avatar. It reacts to what the agent is doing, so you can tell at a glance whether it's thinking, replying, running a tool or waiting for you.

## Where they appear

- **Chat header:** the open chat's agent. This is the one that animates.
- **Latest reply:** the avatar next to the newest assistant message shows the same state as a still pose, with its tool badge. Older messages show the pet idle.
- **Sidebar:** each agent's section header shows its pet, still. It raises a paw when one of the agent's chats needs an approval, and looks busy while a run is going.

## Characters

There are 19 creatures:

- **Blob:** a fluffy hooded plush.
- **Owl:** chubby, with ear tufts and a tiny beak.
- **Rock:** a boxy pebble.
- **Sprout:** a little body with a leaf growing on top.
- **Cat:** pointed ears that flick.
- **Bunny:** long ears, one of which flops over.
- **Bear:** round ears and a little snout.
- **Frog:** big eyes on top of its head.
- **Fox:** tall ears and a pale muzzle.
- **Mouse:** big round pink ears.
- **Penguin:** a white face and orange feet.
- **Chick:** a tuft on top and a tiny beak.
- **Pig:** a round snout and pointy ears.
- **Ghost:** a friendly sheet with a wavy hem.
- **Mushroom:** a spotted cap.
- **Cloud:** a puffy cloud.
- **Axolotl:** frilly pink gills that ripple.
- **Hedgehog:** a coat of quills that bristle.
- **Octopus:** little tentacles that curl.

Each pet also gets an accessory (none, a hat, glasses, a flower clip, a bow or an antenna) and a palette (cream, apricot, moss, stone, peach, lilac, sky or lemon). All three are picked from the agent's identity name (or its id if it has no name), so different agents get different pets, and each keeps the same look on all your devices. Changing an agent's name in [Agents](../agents/#editing-an-agent) gives it a new pet unless you've picked a **Character** for it.

## What the poses mean

| The agent is… | The pet… |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | Blinks now and then. |
| Thinking | Wiggles, with a "…" bubble above its head. |
| Replying | Sways along while the text streams in. |
| Running a tool | Twitches its ears, gills, antenna, leaf or tufts (or wiggles) and shows a badge for the tool, such as a terminal for commands or a magnifying glass for search. |
| Waiting for approval | Raises a paw and waves. |
| Finished | Does a happy hop for about 1.5 seconds. |
| Hit an error | Wobbles worriedly for about 3 seconds. |
| Compacting its context | Dozes off, with a small "z". |

If several things are happening at once, approvals win, then errors, tools, replying, thinking and compacting.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Appearance**:

- **Animated avatars** turns the pets on or off. It's on by default. When it's off, Pincer shows the usual agent avatar.
- **Style** picks **Pixel** (crisp pixel art) or **Plush** (soft and rounded).
- **Character** lets you pick a creature for each agent, or leave it on **Auto** to use the one picked from its identity.

**Style** and **Character** sync to your other devices through the Gateway, so an agent looks the same on your Mac and iPhone. **Animated avatars** is set separately on each device. See [Synced preferences](../../reference/synced-preferences/).

Whenever the agent isn't idle, a soft ring and glow in your theme's accent color appear behind the pet, and the tool badge uses the same accent. The creature's own colors stay the same in every theme. See [Appearance](../appearance/).

## Motion and battery

- With **Reduce Motion** turned on in your system's accessibility settings, each state shows a still pose instead of animating. A working chat's sidebar avatar stays still and shows a small activity dot.
- In a chat, only the header's pet animates, and only while it's visible and Pincer is in the foreground. It redraws only when its pose changes, at no more than about 10 frames a second, and when idle it just blinks now and then.

## VoiceOver

VoiceOver reads the agent's name and what it's doing, for example "Nova is running a tool: exec" or "Nova is waiting for your approval". It also announces when the agent starts waiting for an approval or runs into a problem.
