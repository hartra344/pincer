The **Channels** page shows every channel account on your gateway (Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp and the rest) and whether it's working. You can reconnect, start, stop or log out an account, and link WhatsApp with a QR code, without a terminal.

This page is about the accounts themselves. To let a new person message your agents on a channel, see [Pairing Requests](../gateway-settings/#pairing-requests).

## Opening it

Open [Gateway Settings](../gateway-settings/) and choose **Channels** in the sidebar, just below **Health**. The row gets an attention mark when a channel account has an issue you haven't [dismissed](../gateway-health/#dismissing-issues).

From the [Health page](../gateway-health/), a channel account issue's context menu has **Show in Channels**, which opens this page at that account.

## What's on the page

Accounts are grouped by channel. Each row shows:

- the account (**Default** for the channel's default account);
- its status;
- its last error, if it has one;
- its last activity ("Last message 5 min ago", or "No activity yet").

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The account is running and connected. |
| **Connecting…** | The account is starting up. |
| **Degraded** | It's running but not connected, or it reported an error. The last error says why. |
| **Stopped** | It's set up and enabled but not running. |
| **Needs Login** | It isn't linked yet, or it was logged out. For WhatsApp, [link it with a QR code](#linking-with-a-qr-code). |
| **Disabled** | It's turned off in the gateway's config. |

If no channel is set up yet, the page says **No Channels** and points you to the [setup wizard](../../getting-started/setup-wizard/).

## Refreshing and probing

The page loads when it opens and updates live when the gateway reports a health change. Choose **Refresh** in the toolbar (⌘R on the Mac), or pull down on iOS, to refresh now.

**Probe** asks each account to check its connection with the service right now. It can take several seconds, and anything that fails shows up as the account's last error.

## Managing an account

:::caution[Changing accounts needs Full Management]
Reconnecting, starting, stopping, logging out and QR linking need this device's **Access** to be **Full Management** on the **Connection** page. Without it, the page is read-only and shows **Needs Full Management**: "This device can view but not change this. Open Connection to request Full Management." See [Access levels](../../getting-started/connect-a-gateway/#access-levels).
:::

Use the account's menu button or context menu on the Mac. On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold the account or swipe left on it.

| Action | What it does | Asks first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reconnect** | Stops the account and starts it again, then refreshes. Often fixes a Degraded account. | No |
| **Start** | Starts a stopped account. | No |
| **Stop…** | Stops the account until you start it again or the gateway restarts. Messages to it go unanswered meanwhile. | Yes |
| **Log Out…** | Removes the account's saved login. You'll need to link it again. Only for channels that support it, like WhatsApp. | Yes |
| **Link with QR Code…** | Links an account that needs login. **Relink with QR Code…** for one that's already connected. | No |

While an action runs, the account shows its progress and its buttons are disabled. If the gateway refuses, the reason shows on the account.

## Linking with a QR code

For channels that log in with a QR code, like WhatsApp:

1. Choose **Link with QR Code…**. Pincer shows "Getting a QR code…", then the code.
2. Scan it with the app on your phone (in WhatsApp: **Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device**). If the code changes before you scan it, Pincer shows the new one.
3. When the phone is linked, Pincer says so, closes the code and refreshes the account.

Choose **Cancel** to stop waiting. If linking fails, Pincer shows why and offers **Try Again**.

The [setup wizard](../../getting-started/setup-wizard/)'s Channels step uses the same QR linking.

## Reconnecting from Health

A channel account that isn't running, isn't connected or reported an error shows up as an issue on the [Health page](../gateway-health/). Its context menu (swipe or touch and hold on iOS) has:

- **Reconnect Account**, the same as **Reconnect** here (with Full Management). If the account comes back, the issue clears after the refresh. If it's still failing, the issue stays with the new error.
- **Show in Channels**, which opens this page at that account.

## In the demo

The [demo](../../getting-started/try-the-demo/) has a connected Discord account, a Degraded Telegram account with a connection error, and a WhatsApp account that needs login. Every action, including QR linking, is simulated and works with the default access.

## Current limits

- Channels has been tested against the demo and the [mock gateway](../../development/mock-gateway/) only.
- The gateway has no channel-specific event, so the page updates on health changes, when it opens and when you refresh.
- Some channels can't be started, stopped or logged out at runtime. Pincer hides the actions the gateway says a channel doesn't support.
- Adding a new channel account is done in Gateway Settings or the setup wizard, not on this page.
