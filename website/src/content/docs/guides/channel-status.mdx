**Channel Status** shows every channel account on your gateway (Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp and the rest) and whether it's working. You can reconnect, start, stop or log out an account, and link WhatsApp with a QR code, without a terminal.

This page is about whether accounts are connected. Their settings are on the **Channels** settings page, and letting a new person message your agents is under [Pairing Requests](../gateway-settings/#pairing-requests).

## Opening it

Open [Gateway Settings](../gateway-settings/) and choose **Channel Status** in the sidebar, just below **Health**. The row gets an attention mark when a channel account needs a look.

From the [Health page](../gateway-health/), **Show in Channel Status** in the context menu of a channel account issue, or of a channel in its **Channels** list, opens this page at that account and highlights it.

## What's on the page

Accounts are grouped by channel. Each one shows its state, its last error if it has one (click or tap it to see all of it), any problem the gateway reports with a suggested fix, and when it last sent or received a message ("No activity yet" if it hasn't), since when it's been connected, when it was last probed, and how many times it has tried to reconnect.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The account is running and connected. |
| **Running** | It's running, and the channel doesn't report a connection. |
| **Degraded** | It's running but reported an error. The last error says why. |
| **Disconnected** | It's running but not connected. |
| **Logged Out** | It has no saved login. For WhatsApp, [link it with a QR code](#linking-with-a-qr-code). |
| **Stopped** | It's set up and enabled but not running. |
| **Not Configured** | It's missing settings it needs. Add them on the **Channels** settings page. |
| **Disabled** | It's turned off in the gateway's config. |

Channels are optional: Pincer talks to your agents directly. If none is set up, the page just says **No Channels** (with **Open Channels Settings**); it's never flagged as a problem. If your gateway doesn't report channel status, the page says **Unavailable on this Gateway** and shows the channels from Health, read-only. While Pincer isn't connected, it shows the last list it had, dimmed.

At the bottom, **Approving new senders? See Pairing Requests.** opens [Pairing Requests](../gateway-settings/#pairing-requests).

## Refreshing and probing

The page loads when it opens, updates when the gateway reports a health change, and refreshes every 30 seconds while it's showing. Use **Refresh** in the toolbar, or pull down on iOS, to refresh now.

**Probe** asks each channel to check its connection with the service now. It can take several seconds ("Probing channels…"), and anything that fails shows up on the account. If some channels don't answer in time, the page says so.

## Managing an account

:::caution[Changing accounts needs Full Management]
Reconnecting, starting, stopping, logging out and QR login need this device's **Access** to be **Full Management** on the **Connection** page. Without it, the page is read-only and shows **Needs Full Management**: "This device can view but not change this. Open Connection to request Full Management." with **Open Connection…**. See [Access levels](../../getting-started/connect-a-gateway/#access-levels).
:::

Use the account's **…** button or its context menu. On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold the account, or swipe left for **Reconnect** and **Stop…**. With VoiceOver, the actions are in the actions rotor.

| Action | What it does | Asks first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reconnect** | Stops the account, then starts it again. Often fixes a Degraded or Disconnected account. | No |
| **Start** | Starts a stopped account. | No |
| **Stop…** | Asks "Stop Telegram (Default)?", then stops the account until you start it again or the gateway restarts. Messages to it won't be answered. | Yes |
| **Log Out…** | Removes the saved login. To reconnect, scan a QR code again (WhatsApp) or set the account up again. | Yes |
| **Link with QR Code…** | Links an account that needs login. **Relink with QR Code…** for one that's already linked. | No |

While an action runs, the account shows it ("Reconnecting…") and its other actions wait. Pincer says when it's done ("Reconnected Telegram."). If it fails, the reason shows on the account ("Couldn't reconnect: …"). When the gateway says a channel can't be started or logged out, Pincer hides that action for the channel from then on.

## Linking with a QR code

For channels that log in with a QR code, like WhatsApp:

1. Choose **Link with QR Code…** on the account. A sheet opens with "Getting a QR code…", then the code.
2. Scan it with the app on your phone (in WhatsApp: **Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device**). If the code changes before you scan it, Pincer shows the new one.
3. When the phone is linked, the sheet says **Linked**, closes and the account refreshes.

Choose **Cancel** to stop waiting. If linking fails, the sheet says why and offers **Try Again**.

## Reconnecting from Health

A channel account that isn't running, isn't connected or reported an error shows up as an issue on the [Health page](../gateway-health/). Its context menu has:

- **Reconnect Account**, the same as **Reconnect** here. For a logged-out account that links with a QR code, like WhatsApp, it's **Link with QR Code…** instead, which opens the same QR sheet. On iOS you can also swipe right on the issue, and VoiceOver has it in the actions rotor. Without Full Management it shows as locked, for example **Reconnect Account (Needs Full Management)**. The issue shows "Reconnecting…" while it runs and clears after the refresh if the account comes back. If it's still failing, the issue stays and says why.
- **Show in Channel Status**, which opens this page at that account.

## In the demo

The [demo](../../getting-started/try-the-demo/) has a connected Discord account, a degraded Telegram account with a connection error, and a logged-out WhatsApp account. Every action, including QR linking, is simulated and works with the default access. Telegram's error clears with **Reconnect**, and Discord doesn't support logging out, like a real Discord account.

## Current limits

- Channel Status has been tested against the demo and the [mock gateway](../../development/mock-gateway/) only.
- The gateway has no channel-specific event, so the page updates on health changes, when it opens and when you refresh.
- Some channels can't be started or logged out at runtime. Pincer only finds out when the gateway says so, then hides that action.
- Adding a channel account is done on the **Channels** settings page.
