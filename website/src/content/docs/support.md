Pincer is completely free. There are no Pincer subscriptions or in-app purchases.
Live use requires your own OpenClaw Gateway; any hosting or AI-provider charges
come from the services you choose, not from Pincer.

## Try Pincer without a gateway

Choose **Try the Demo** on the welcome screen. The demo runs on your device and
needs no account, credentials, Tailscale or hosted backend. See
[Try the demo](../getting-started/try-the-demo/) for sample conversations and
features to explore.

## Get help

- Start with [Troubleshooting](../reference/troubleshooting/) for connection,
  permissions and gateway compatibility problems.
- Follow [Connect a gateway](../getting-started/connect-a-gateway/) for live setup.
- [Search existing issues](https://github.com/hartra344/pincer/issues) or
  [open a support issue](https://github.com/hartra344/pincer/issues/new) to contact
  the maintainer about a bug, question or privacy-policy concern.

When reporting a problem, include the Pincer version, device model, OS version,
whether it occurs in the demo, steps to reproduce, and what you expected to happen.
GitHub issues are public: remove credentials, gateway addresses you want to keep
private, personal conversations and other sensitive information from screenshots
or logs before posting. Do not include personal data in a privacy-policy question.

## Privacy and local data

Read the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for recipients, retention and user choices,
or [Security & privacy](../reference/security/) for technical details. The Pincer
developer does not operate your gateway and cannot retrieve or erase its history.
