Once Pincer is [connected to your gateway](../connect-a-gateway/), choose **Continue Setup** to finish getting ready to chat. There are three short steps, and you can skip any of them:

1. **Agent & Model**: pick the agent and model new chats start with.
2. **Skills** (optional): see what extra abilities your agent has.
3. **Test Message**: send a message to your agent and make sure it answers.

Setup doesn't replace any settings. Each step shows a summary and links to the page where you'd change it. When you're done, you land in your chat list with the gateway selected.

## Start or resume setup

On a new gateway, setup follows straight on from [connecting](../connect-a-gateway/). You can also open it any time the gateway is connected:

- choose **Set Up Gateway…** from the gateway's menu in the sidebar;
- press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Set Up Gateway…** (dimmed while the gateway is disconnected). Until setup is complete, it shows how many steps are settled;
- open [Gateway Settings](../../guides/gateway-settings/) → **Overview** and choose **Set Up Gateway…**. It shows **Done** once setup is complete.

Pincer remembers each gateway's progress separately, including after you relaunch. Setup opens on the first step that isn't done or skipped yet.

Use the buttons at the bottom:

- **Continue** goes to the next step. On the last step it's **Finish**.
- **Skip** moves on without changing anything.
- **Back** goes to the previous step.
- **Close** keeps your progress for later. When setup follows on from connecting, **Skip to Chats** takes its place at the top and goes straight to your chats.

## Agent & Model

Pick a **Default agent** and **Default model**, then choose **Save Defaults**. Pincer saves them to the gateway: the agent's `agents.entries.<id>.default` and `agents.defaults.model.primary`. If Gateway Settings has unsaved changes, save or discard those first. For anything more, such as adding agents or per-agent models, choose **Open Agents & Models**.

Saving needs **Full Management**. Without it, the step shows **Needs Full Management**: "This device can view but not change this. Open Connection to request Full Management." You can still see the current defaults, or skip the step. Choose **Open Connection…** to change the access level (see [Access levels](../connect-a-gateway/#access-levels)).

## Skills (optional)

Skills add abilities to your agent, and you can add them any time. This step lists your agent's skills, and marks any that aren't set up yet, such as one waiting for a CLI tool. That's for information only: a skill that isn't set up never counts as a problem, and you can continue straight past it. **Open Skills** takes you to the [Skills page](../../guides/skills-and-tools/) if you want to set one up now.

## Test message

The message starts out as "Hi! Are you there?" Choose **Send**, or type your own and press Return. As the step says, "Pincer starts a chat called Setup Test with Claw" (or whichever agent is your default). It starts that chat the first time and reuses it after that. While it waits, setup shows "Sent. Waiting for your agent…". Choose **Send Again** to send another.

When the agent answers, setup shows **Your agent replied.** with the first few lines of the reply, and "Finish opens this chat." Choose **Finish** to land in your chat list with the *Setup Test* chat selected, ready to keep talking. If the agent doesn't answer, setup says "Your agent didn't answer. You can try again, or skip and chat later." Choose **Try Again** or **Skip**.

## Tips

After setup closes, Pincer shows **Tips** once, after a short delay so you can read the chat first. On Mac and full-width iPad, Tips opens in a sheet and takes keyboard and VoiceOver focus; choose **Got It** or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close it. On iPhone, it appears as a short strip at the bottom of the chat list. It covers slash commands, `/think`, approvals, the command palette (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), Find in Chat (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>) and searching all your messages. On iPad, the keyboard tips are marked *(iPad keyboard)*; on iPhone they're left out.

On iPhone the card is a short strip at the bottom of the chat list, above **Find a chat**, so your chats stay visible. It steps aside while a chat is open, so it never covers the composer. It shows one tip at a time: tap **Next Tip** to see the next one, or **Got It** to close it at any point.

If iOS or macOS is asking whether Pincer may send notifications, the card waits until you've answered.

To see it again, choose **Show Tips Again** under **Settings → General → Tips**. The card comes back the next time the main window is open.

## Try it in the demo

**Try the Demo** goes straight to the chat list, but you can open setup in the [demo](../try-the-demo/) from its gateway menu or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> → **Set Up Gateway…**. It never saves your progress, so you can try it as often as you like. The demo connects with standard access, so **Agent & Model** shows **Needs Full Management**, and **Test Message** sends to a demo *Setup Test* chat and gets a demo reply. The demo shows the tips card again each time Pincer launches.
